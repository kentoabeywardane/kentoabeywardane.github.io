3D molecular alignment with ShEPhERD-score

Aligning molecules in 3D to measure their similarity is a core operation in cheminformatics. ShEPhERD-score provides differentiable 3D similarity functions for scoring and alignment across shape, electrostatic potential (ESP), pharmacophores, and their combinations. Below, we demonstrate the rigid alignment of two molecules using three representations: volumetric shape, solvent-accessible surface, and pharmacophores.

Bioisosteric fragment merging with ShEPhERD-2

An interaction profile comprises a molecule's 3D shape, electrostatics, and pharmacophores. ShEPhERD-2 learns the joint distribution of a molecule and its interaction profile which enables practitioners to design molecules conditioned on a desired set of interactions.

One application is bioisosteric fragment merging. Fragment soaking screens identify weak binders which must be evolved into larger, druglike molecules. In this example, we demonstrate how interaction profiles from multiple fragments can be composed to condition ShEPhERD-2 and generate a single molecule that recapitulates the interactions of the original fragments. In our preprint, we demonstrate how this approach can utilize computational feedback from docking or co-folding for optimization.

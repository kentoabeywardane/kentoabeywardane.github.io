About Me

I'm a Chemical Engineering and Computation PhD candidate at MIT currently in the Coley Research Group working on 3D generative molecular design. I have primarily focused on developing interaction-aware generative models to improve interaction recapitulation and programmatic design of generated molecules. I have also developed, and continue to maintain, a suite of tools for 3D rigid alignment and similarity scoring using 3D molecular representations of interactions. I am passionate about developing tools that can help translate computational designs into real-world applications.

Previously, I graduated with a Master's in Data-Enabled Computational Engineering and Sciences (DECES) and a Bachelor's in Chemical Engineering at Brown University where I worked in the Goldsmith Lab for Chemical Kinetics. My master's thesis was a culmination of my undergraduate DFT and coupled cluster work with PFOA and GenX compounds where I developed a package (autoCBH) that automatically computes accurate heats of formation for large molecules by utilizing the error-cancelling connectivity-based hierarchy (CBH) method.

Featured Work

ShEPhERD-2

Interactive demo Paper Code Weights

Interaction profiles as a universal language for generative molecular design

Samples diverse molecules conditioned on a target interaction profile, with precise control through pharmacophore prioritization, substructure constraints, and the composition of multiple profiles. Enables iterative optimization for bioisosteric fragment merging, dual-target design, selectivity engineering, and peptide-to-small-molecule modality hopping.

ShEPhERD-score

Interactive demo Docs PyPI Code

Fast, adaptable 3D alignment and scoring of molecular features

The measuring stick for the rest of the family: conformer generation, interaction-profile extraction, differentiable 3D similarity, and alignment in PyTorch and JAX, together with the evaluation suite used to benchmark 3D generative models.

ShEPhERD ICLR 2025 Oral

Paper arXiv Code

Diffusing shape, electrostatics, and pharmacophores for bioisosteric drug design

An SE(3)-equivariant diffusion model over the joint distribution of 3D molecular structures and their interaction profiles comprising shape, electrostatics, and pharmacophores.
